# Original Doll scene and encounter entry

This continuation replaces the sister-room development trigger boundary with the seven original `Podunk/cutscenes/doll_attack.yaml` phrases. It is a source-content extension of the shared core, not a general GDScript interpreter. Room and House schema3 packs carry actor profiles, movement paths, source text, animation keys, command order, camera requests, flags and encounter references.

The source handler order is explicit: teleport precedes movement even when YAML lists it afterward. Position/STEP paths contain vectors and waits; each step computes its target when it starts. Movement is asynchronous, moonwalk changes visual facing, and an older timer may resume while a newer turn or motion is active. A SceneTreeTimer stores its countdown as float32; Timer nodes have their separately verified behavior. Source text confirmations advance the script while the close animation continues.

Mimmie and Doll replace the original visible NPCs during the scene. The actor copy has its source sheet, shadow policy, sprite offsets, Float/Walk/Talk tracks and emote. Returning an ordinary NPC copies pose first, then waits for a real frame_changed signal and another idle_frame; an unchanged Idle frame can leave cleanup waiting. No timeout invents a signal. Each actor camera retains independent tweens and shakes across Doll→Mimmie→Doll; the source screen-center and limits transfer on camera change.

The final phrase stops the selected room shaker, sets `doll_attack`, queues the Doll and records `actorskeep=true` with post-win `Podunk/cutscenes/doll_defeated`. The encounter begins only when the cutscene ends. Neither `doll_defeated` nor `doll_melody` is granted by entry. The original Doll has its own checked battle pack, sprite, Baby background and boss intro request. Prior Lamp HP/PP and rewards remain session state; the entry portrait reflects retained HP/PP. Doll combat actions, level-up, post-win world restoration and melody are the next stage.

The Baby shader adds source scroll, ping-pong, compression and palette behavior. Its source omits the enabled palette divisor. The explicit external compatibility policy is limited to the observed pinned Godot3.6.2 GLES2 output on llvmpipe: fixed palette row0, with evidence/source hashes required. It does not fabricate a divisor of4 or promise undefined arithmetic behaves identically on every GPU. Details in DOLL_ENTRY.md and reports/doll-background-reference.

Reference coverage includes native actor and multi-camera cases at both400×240 and320×180, parser negative cases, source-derived text and a shared-core run from real Lamp victory through both Mimmie lines to the Doll command menu. Individual mechanism traces do not prove complete original-game framebuffer equivalence. Current platform validation and performance limits are documented in [STATUS](STATUS.md).
